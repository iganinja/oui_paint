# Third-party pieces

| Piece | What it does here | License | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| OUI (Olagarro) | the interface, windows and scripting | zlib | `third_party/olagarro/LICENSE` |
| stb_image, stb_image_write | read images; write JPEG, BMP and TGA | MIT or public domain | `third_party/stb/LICENSE` |
| LodePNG | write PNG, also at 16 bits | zlib | `third_party/lodepng/LICENSE` |
| miniz | zip OpenRaster files | MIT | `third_party/miniz/LICENSE` |
| Godot editor icons | the icons | MIT | `icons/godot/LICENSE.txt` |

OUI brings its own: QuickJS-ng, stb_truetype, NanoSVG, SDL3 and its fonts, listed in
`third_party/olagarro/licenses/README.md`.
